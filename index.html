How to Play

Goal

Get rid of all your cards. The last player still holding cards is the Shithead — and must make tea.

Setup

  • Each player gets 3 face-down cards (cannot be looked at).
  • On top of those, 3 face-up cards everyone can see.
  • Plus 3 hand cards that only you can see.
  • Before play, you may swap any hand card with a face-up card to set yourself up.

Play

  • Whoever holds the lowest non-special card starts.
  • Single-card play: a normal card plays if it's (a) a higher rank in the same suit (any gap is fine — e.g., 9♠ on 3♠), or (b) the same rank in any suit. Specials have their own rules.
  • Multi-card plays use chain rules. Each new card must chain to the previous one by either equal rank in any suit, OR strictly consecutive in the same suit (next rank up or down — no jumps). Same-rank pairs, runs (up or down), and King chains can all be mixed: K♠ → 7♠ → 7♥, or a run 3♥ → 4♥ → 5♥, or 5♠ + 5♥ + 6♥ + 7♥. Click the cards in chain order. Note: 5♠ → 3♠ is not allowed in a chain because the run isn't consecutive — chains require ±1.
  • Jacks have their own rule: a Jack only plays on another Jack or on a lower rank in the same suit.
  • While the deck has cards, refill your hand back to 3 after each play. Hand and face-up are locked away until the deck is empty. Only when your hand is empty AND the deck is empty can you play face-up cards. Then face-down (blindly), one at a time.
  • If you can't play (and won't), pick up the pile. If a blind face-down can't play, pick up the pile + the flipped card.

Magic Cards

  • 2 — Wild lead + pickup +2. A 2 of any suit plays on anything on top, same as a 10. The only exception is when the 8 skip queue is alive — only another 8 plays then. The next player must pick up 2 from the deck unless they counter (see chain). Stacking another 2 passes the additive total down the chain. In a hand chain, a 2 is NOT wild — it must follow numerical sequence (pair, ±1 same suit, or A↔2 low-Ace). Exception: a 2 may chain off a black Jack to extend the pickup chain.
  • 8 — Skip stack. An 8 only plays on another 8 or on a lower rank in the same suit. Each 8 stacks one skip in the queue — two 8s = the next two players miss their go; three 8s = three miss. While the queue is alive, only another 8 plays. Once the queue drains, the 8 sitting on top is just a normal card and the following player plays per the usual rules (e.g., a higher-same-suit card). With wraparound, the player who played the 8s can get another go after everyone else is skipped.
  • 7 — Lower-or-equal lock. A 7 plays per the normal rule (it is not wild). When a 7 is on top, the next player must play 7 or lower (any card 2–7 of any suit — suit/rank rule overridden) or a 10 (wild, burns). 8 / 9 / J / Q / K / A are blocked on a 7. (9 is now an ordinary number card.)
  • 10 — Burn (wild). A 10 of any suit can go on anything — even a pickup chain — to clear the pack. (Still rejected while skips from an 8 are queued.) In a chain, however, a 10 must be in numerical sequence like any other card.
  • J — Jack. A Jack only plays on another Jack or on a lower rank in the same suit. Black Jacks (♠ ♣) add +5 to a pickup chain; red Jacks (♥ ♦) cancel the most recent black Jack.
  • Q — Reverse & lock. A Queen can only be played on another Queen or in suit. Flips direction of play (two players: you go again). Once on top, it can only be followed by another Queen, a higher rank in the Q's suit (K or A), a 2, a 10, or an Ace.
  • K — Royal demand. A King can only be played on another King or in suit. Exceptions: a K cannot follow a freshly-placed 8 or J — the previous player must have had a turn to react first. A K can follow a freshly-placed 2, but only if it's the same suit. When chaining off a K in your own play, the legal followers are: another K (pair), any card of the K's suit, a 10 of any suit (burns), or the same-suit Ace (high-Ace ±1). Solo King? +1 card from the deck.
  • A — Wild lead, suit-pivot in chains. An Ace of any suit plays at any time except after a 2, black Jack, or 8. Click an Ace and you'll be asked to name the called suit immediately. In a chain, the Ace pivots the chain to the called suit and can be high (14) or low (1) — so the legal next link is another Ace (pair), the K of the called suit (high), or the 2 of the called suit (low). Example: A♥ called as ♠ → K♠ or 2♠ continues the chain.
  • Four of a kind on the discard — burns the pile. Same player plays again.

The Pickup Chain

Once a 2 (or a black Jack) is played, a chain begins. The next player must either counter or take the cards from the deck:

  • 2 — adds +2 to the running total.
  • Black Jack (♠ or ♣) — adds +5.
  • Red Jack (♥ or ♦) — cancels the most recent black Jack and its 5 cards. Other 2s and black Jacks in the chain still apply.
  • If you can't or won't counter, click Take from Deck — you pick up the running total from the deck, the chain ends.
  • Run-out rule: if a player's multi-card play mixes chain cards (2 / Jacks) with a run and ends on a non-chain card (e.g., 2♥ → 3♥ → 4♥), the pickup chain is cancelled. The next player just plays on the last card normally.

The ? on each card explains what it does. Shitstein is a Frankenstein of Shithead (face-down / face-up / hand structure, 10 burns, four-of-a-kind burns, last-one-out loses) and UK Blackjack / Switch (2 / black-J / red-J pickup chain, 8 skip, Q reverse, A names suit), with a 7 lower-or-equal lock. Common Shithead variants not in play: 8 = invisible, 3 = mirror.
